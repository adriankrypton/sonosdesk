**The first public release of SonosDesk** – control your Sonos speakers from the Windows desktop
and the notification area, locally and without a cloud account.

## Download

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **`SonosDesk-Setup-0.1.0-win-x64.exe`** | Recommended. Installs SonosDesk for your user account – no administrator rights needed. Start menu entry, optional desktop icon and "Start with Windows". |
| `SonosDesk-0.1.0-win-x64-portable.exe` | Portable single file. Run it from any folder, nothing is installed. |
| `SonosDesk-0.1.0-win-x64-portable.zip` | The portable version with license and readme. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Checksums to verify the downloads (`Get-FileHash <file>` in PowerShell). |

Requires Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit, and Sonos speakers (S1 or S2) in the same
network. No .NET runtime needs to be installed.

> SonosDesk is not code-signed yet. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click
> **More info → Run anyway**.

## What you can do

**Play and listen**
- Play/pause, next/previous, seek within the track, shuffle, repeat, crossfade
- Title, artist, album and cover – also for music started from the Spotify app (**Spotify Connect**)
- **Queue:** see what comes next, play from any track, remove tracks, clear it
- **Sleep timer** from 15 to 120 minutes with countdown
- **Sonos favorites** and **internet radio** from the open Radio Browser directory: search, genres,
  countries, *My stations* with folders, your own logos and your own stream URLs. For radio, the song
  that is on air right now is shown instead of a stream name.

**Volume and sound**
- Volume per room and for whole groups, mute
- **Volume profiles** (e.g. "Evening", "Party") that **fade smoothly** to the new volume – the fade time
  is adjustable in the settings (0–10 s)
- **Sound per room:** bass, treble, loudness, and night sound / speech enhancement on soundbars

**Rooms and groups**
- Group and ungroup rooms with one click, or let all rooms play together (**party mode**)
- **Group composer:** pick speakers, something to play and a volume in one go
- **Areas:** saved, colored room combinations such as "Downstairs"
- Play on a whole group, on a single speaker of a group, or on several rooms selected with Ctrl+click
- **Speaker info:** right-click a speaker → *Info* for IP address, host name, model, software and
  hardware version, MAC address and serial number

**Windows integration**
- Tray icon with a compact flyout (left click) and a full menu (right click); SonosDesk keeps running
  when the window is closed
- Keyboard **media keys** and the Windows media overlay control the active room
- Global hotkeys, quick actions and schedules (e.g. "weekdays at 7:00 – radio in the kitchen")
- Light and dark theme, start with Windows, rearrangeable start page
- 8 languages: English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Русский
- **Update hint:** future versions are announced in the app (can be switched off)

## Good to know

- On the first start, allow SonosDesk for **private networks** when Windows asks about the firewall.
  If no speakers appear (VPN, guest Wi-Fi, separate VLAN), right-click the room list →
  *Add speaker by IP address*.
- Your settings are stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SonosDesk`. Uninstalling asks whether to delete them.
- No telemetry, no account. Internet connections are only made for covers and station logos, the
  radio directory, the optional update check and the optional Sonos cloud backend.

## Known limitations

- The playback state is refreshed every 2 seconds instead of being pushed by the speakers.
- No music library browsing and no alarms yet.
- The optional Sonos Control API (cloud) backend is experimental and has fewer features than the
  default local mode.

Feedback and bug reports are welcome in the [issues](https://github.com/adriankrypton/sonosdesk/issues).

### Was SonosDesk kann

- Abspielen/Pause, vor/zurück, spulen, Zufall, Wiederholen, Überblenden – auch bei **Spotify Connect**
- **Warteschlange**, **Schlaf-Timer** (15–120 Minuten), **Sonos-Favoriten** und **Internetradio** mit
  *Meine Sender*, Ordnern, eigenen Logos und eigenen Stream-URLs; beim Radio wird der laufende Song angezeigt
- Lautstärke pro Raum und Gruppe; **Lautstärkeprofile blenden sanft über** (Dauer einstellbar, 0–10 s)
- **Klang pro Raum:** Bässe, Höhen, Loudness, bei Soundbars Nachtmodus und Sprachverbesserung
- Gruppieren mit einem Klick, **Party-Modus**, Gruppen-Assistent, farbige **Bereiche**, Mehrfachauswahl mit Strg
- **Lautsprecher-Info:** Rechtsklick → *Info* zeigt IP-Adresse, Hostname, Modell, Software-/Hardware-Version,
  MAC-Adresse und Seriennummer
- Symbol im Infobereich mit Flyout und Menü, **Medientasten**, Windows-Medienanzeige, globale Tastenkürzel,
  Schnellaktionen, Zeitpläne, helles/dunkles Design, 8 Sprachen, **Update-Hinweis** für künftige Versionen

### Gut zu wissen

- Beim ersten Start in der Windows-Firewall den Zugriff für **private Netzwerke** erlauben. Werden keine
  Lautsprecher gefunden (VPN, Gast-WLAN, VLAN): Rechtsklick in die Raumliste →
  *Lautsprecher per IP-Adresse hinzufügen*.
- Einstellungen liegen in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SonosDesk`; beim Deinstallieren wirst du gefragt, ob sie gelöscht werden.
- Keine Telemetrie, kein Konto.

### Bekannte Einschränkungen

- Der Wiedergabestatus wird alle 2 Sekunden abgefragt.
- Noch keine Musikbibliothek und keine Wecker.
- Das optionale Cloud-Backend (Sonos Control API) ist experimentell und kann weniger als der lokale Modus.

Feedback und Fehlerberichte gerne in den [Issues](https://github.com/adriankrypton/sonosdesk/issues) – auch auf Deutsch.
